### Day 1 — Establish your baseline

- 10 min: Write 10 sentences about your life without using a translator.
- 15 min: Learn these chunks:
    - "I usually spend my evenings..."
    - "I've been getting into..."
    - "I decided to try..."
    - "I ended up..."
    - "I'm planning to..."
- 15 min: Study the basic sentence pattern: subject + verb + object/complement.
- 20 min: Write 10 sentences about your typical day. Read them aloud.

- 15 min: Listen to a beginner-friendly conversation about daily routines.
- 15 min: Shadow 5–8 short sentences.
- 20 min: Record a 60–90-second introduction about yourself, your job, hobbies, and typical weekend.
- 10 min: Note your five most noticeable mistakes.

These are very useful **English chunks** (common sentence patterns) for talking about your hobbies, habits, and plans.

### 1. "I usually spend my evenings..."

**Meaning:** I often use my evenings doing something.

**Pattern:**

> I usually spend my evenings + V-ing / activity.

**Examples:**

- I usually spend my evenings **watching movies**.
- I usually spend my evenings **reading books**.
- I usually spend my evenings **studying English**.

✅ Full sentence:

> I usually spend my evenings studying English and listening to podcasts.

---

### 2. "I've been getting into..."

**Meaning:** I've recently started to like or become interested in something.

**Pattern:**

> I've been getting into + hobby/topic/activity.

**Examples:**

- I've been getting into **photography**.
- I've been getting into **yoga** lately.
- I've been getting into **Korean dramas**.

✅ Full sentence:

> I've been getting into photography, and it's a lot of fun.

---

### 3. "I decided to try..."

**Meaning:** I made a choice to do something new.

**Pattern:**

> I decided to try + noun / verb.

**Examples:**

- I decided to try **painting**.
- I decided to try **learning Japanese**.
- I decided to try **a new sport**.

✅ Full sentence:

> I decided to try learning Japanese because I love Japanese culture.

---

### 4. "I ended up..."

**Meaning:** This is what happened in the end, often unexpectedly.

**Pattern:**

> I ended up + V-ing.

**Examples:**

- I ended up **buying the book**.
- I ended up **joining a football club**.
- I ended up **staying home all weekend**.

✅ Full sentence:

> I planned to go out, but I ended up staying home and watching Netflix.

---

### 5. "I'm planning to..."

**Meaning:** I intend to do something in the future.

**Pattern:**

> I'm planning to + verb.

**Examples:**

- I'm planning to **start a business**.
- I'm planning to **travel to Japan next year**.
- I'm planning to **take an English course**.

✅ Full sentence:

> I'm planning to improve my speaking skills by practicing every day.

---
