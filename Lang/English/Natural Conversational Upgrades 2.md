# [[English-Daily]] - 10 Natural Conversational Upgrades

## 🎯 1. Thay vì nói: "I'll pay the bill" (Tôi sẽ trả tiền)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"It’s on me."** / **"My treat."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "Put your wallet away, **it's on me** today!"
    - "Let's go grab some coffee, **my treat**."

## 🎯 2. Thay vì nói: "I agree with you" (Tôi đồng ý)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I'm with you on that."** / **"I feel you."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "This traffic is insane. — **I'm with you on that**, bro."
    - "I need a vacation. — **I feel you**, man."

## 🎯 3. Thay vì nói: "I'm listening to you" (Tôi đang nghe nè)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I'm all ears."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "Do you have a minute? I have a story. — Go ahead, **I'm all ears**."
    - "Tell me what happened, **I'm all ears**."

## 🎯 4. Thay vì nói: "I don't know" (Tôi không biết - nghe hơi cụt ngủn)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I have no clue."** / **"Beats me."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "Why is the server down? — **I have no clue**, let me check."
    - "Where are my keys? — **Beats me**, I haven't seen them."

## 🎯 5. Thay vì nói: "I'll come to your place" (Tôi sẽ đến chỗ ông)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I'll swing by."** / **"I'll drop by."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "I'll **swing by** your house after I drop off my mom."
    - "Can I **drop by** your office for a sec?"

## 🎯 6. Thay vì nói: "I'm very hungry" (Tôi rất đói)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I'm starving."** / **"I'm famished."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    
    - "Is dinner ready? **I'm starving**!"
    - "I haven't eaten all day, **I'm famished**."

## 🎯 7. Thay vì nói: "I'll call you back" (Tôi sẽ gọi lại sau)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"I'll get back to you."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "I'm driving right now, **I'll get back to you** later."
    - "Let me check the schedule and **I'll get back to you**."

## 🎯 8. Thay vì nói: "I'm sorry, I forgot" (Xin lỗi tôi quên mất)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"It slipped my mind."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "I was supposed to buy milk, but **it slipped my mind**."
    - "Sorry I didn't call, **it totally slipped my mind**."

## 🎯 9. Thay vì nói: "It's very easy" (Dễ ợt)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"It's a piece of cake."** / **"Nogie."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    - "Fixing this bug was **a piece of cake**."
    - "Can you help me? — Sure, **no biggie**!"

## 🎯 10. Thay vì nói: "Relax" (Thư giãn đi - đôi khi nghe như ra lệnh)

- ✅ **Bản xứ:** **"Chill out."** / **"Take it easy."**
- **Ví dụ:**
    
    - "Don't get so angry, just **chill out**."
    - "You've been working too hard, **take it easy** this weekend."
        

---

## 🛠 Key Vocabulary for Upgrading

- **Starving**: Đói cồn cào (Mạnh hơn _hungry_).
- **Swing by**: Ghé ngang qua (Nghe nhanh gọn, không trịnh trọng).
- **No biggie**: Chuyện nhỏ, không có gì to tát.
- **Get back to someone**: Phản hồi lại ai đó sau.
