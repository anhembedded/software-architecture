# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Abide by

## 🎯 1. Meaning (Nghĩa chính)

**Abide by = tuân theo / làm theo một luật, quy tắc, hoặc thỏa thuận.**

👉 Thường dùng khi nói về:

- rules
- laws
- agreements
- decisions

⚠️ Vibe của từ này: **hơi formal một chút**, nhưng vẫn rất phổ biến trong đời sống và công việc.

---

## 🎯 2. Thay vì nói (cách sách vở / phổ thông)

❌ **follow the rules**  
❌ **obey the rules**

👉 Native thường nói:

✅ **abide by the rules**

---

## 🎯 3. Main Sentence (câu chuẩn)

**Everyone has to abide by the rules.**

---

## 🎯 4. Real Examples

### Workplace / công việc

- "All employees must **abide by the company policy**."
- "If you work here, you have to **abide by the safety rules**."

---

### IT / dev environment (chuẩn gu ông)

- "All contributors need to **abide by the coding standards**."
- "We have to **abide by the API contract**."

---

### Đời sống

- "Drivers must **abide by traffic laws**."
- "If you live in this building, you need to **abide by the house rules**."

---

## 🎯 5. Native Collocations (cực hay đi chung)

- **abide by the law**
- **abide by the rules**
- **abide by the agreement**
- **abide by the contract**
- **abide by the decision**

Ví dụ:

- "Both sides agreed to **abide by the contract**."
- "The team has to **abide by the referee's decision**."

---

## 🎯 6. Natural Conversation Version

Trong nói chuyện thường ngày, người bản xứ đôi khi nói:

👉 **stick to the rules**

Ví dụ:

- "Just **stick to the rules**, man."
- "If everyone **sticks to the plan**, it’ll work."

📌 Vibe:

| Expression | Vibe |
|---|---|
| abide by | formal / professional |
| stick to | casual / spoken |
| follow | neutral |

---

## 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **abide** = chấp nhận / tuân thủ  
- **policy** = chính sách  
- **contract** = hợp đồng  
- **agreement** = thỏa thuận
