# 🎯 1. Check somebody / something out (investigate carefully)

👉 **Check someone/something out = kiểm tra / xem xét kỹ**

Dùng khi:

- tuyển nhân viên
- kiểm tra thông tin
- đánh giá ai đó

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ investigate someone  
❌ examine someone carefully  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **check someone out**

---

### Main Sentence

**The company checks out all new employees.**

---

### More Examples

💼 **Work**

- HR **checks out** all applicants.  
- The police **checked him out** before hiring him.

---

💻 **IT**

- We should **check this library out** before using it.  
- I'll **check that tool out** later.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- Let me **check it out**.  
- I'll **check that out**.

---

# 🎯 2. Check out something (look at / admire – informal)

👉 **Check out = nhìn xem / coi thử**

Rất phổ biến trong spoken English.

---

### Main Sentence

**Check out that guy's crazy hair!**

---

### More Examples

👀 **Đời sống**

- **Check out** that car.  
- **Check out** this restaurant.

---

💻 **Internet**

- **Check out** this website.  
- **Check out** this cool tool.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- Dude, **check this out**.  
- You should **check it out**.
