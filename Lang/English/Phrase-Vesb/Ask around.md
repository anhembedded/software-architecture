# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Ask around

## 🎯 1. Meaning

**Ask around = hỏi nhiều người xung quanh để tìm thông tin.**

👉 Không phải hỏi **một người**.  
👉 Là **đi hỏi nhiều người trong khu vực / nhóm**.

Thường dùng khi:

- tìm đồ bị mất
- tìm thông tin
- hỏi recommendation
- điều tra nhẹ

---

## 🎯 2. Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ **ask many people**  
❌ **ask everyone**

👉 Native thường nói:

✅ **ask around**

---

## 🎯 3. Main Sentence

**I asked around, but nobody has seen my wallet.**

---

## 🎯 4. More Examples

### Đời sống

- I **asked around**, but nobody knows where he went.  
- Let me **ask around** and see if anyone can help.  
- I’ll **ask around** about a good mechanic.

---

### Công việc / IT

- I **asked around** the team, but no one knows why the server crashed.  
- Let me **ask around** and see who owns this module.  
- I’ll **ask around** if anyone has dealt with this bug before.

---

### Hỏi recommendation

- I **asked around** for a good gym.  
- She **asked around** for a good doctor.  
- I **asked around** about a reliable laptop repair shop.

---

## 🎯 5. Native Conversation Lines

Những câu này người bản xứ dùng rất nhiều:

- **Let me ask around.**  
- **I'll ask around and get back to you.**  
- **Ask around and see what people say.**

---

## 🎯 6. Natural Conversation Version

Native đôi khi nói nhanh hơn:

- **I'll check around.**  
- **Let me see what I can find out.**

Ví dụ:

- Let me **ask around** and I'll let you know.  
- I'll **ask around** the office tomorrow.

---

## 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **ask around** → hỏi nhiều người xung quanh  
- **see if** → xem thử có hay không  
- **find out** → tìm ra thông tin
