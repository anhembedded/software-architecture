# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Break in / Break into

## 🎯 1. Break in (force entry – usually a house)

👉 **Break in = đột nhập vào nhà / tòa nhà**

Thường dùng khi nói về:

- burglary
- trộm
- nhà bị đột nhập

⚠️ Native thường nói **break in** hoặc **break into a place**

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ enter the house illegally  
❌ force entry into the house  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **break in**  
✅ **break into the house**

---

### Main Sentence

**Somebody broke in last night and stole our stereo.**

---

### More Examples

🏠 **Đời sống**

- Someone **broke into our house** last week.  
- Thieves **broke in** while we were on vacation.  
- The police think someone **broke in through the window**.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- Our place got **broken into**.  
- Someone **broke in** last night.  
- The house was **broken into**.

---

# 🎯 2. Break into something (enter by force)

👉 **Break into = phá cửa / xông vào để vào bên trong**

Thường dùng khi:

- cứu người
- cảnh sát hành động
- trộm

---

### Main Sentence

**The firemen had to break into the room to rescue the children.**

---

### More Examples

🚒 **Emergency**

- Firefighters **broke into the apartment** to save the dog.  
- Police **broke into the building** during the raid.

---

🏠 **Crime**

- The thieves **broke into the car**.  
- Someone **broke into the office** last night.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- They **broke into the building**.  
- Someone **broke into my car**.  
- Police **broke into the house**.

---

# 🎯 3. Break something in (make new things comfortable)

👉 **Break something in = dùng một thời gian để đồ mới mềm / quen**

Rất hay dùng với:

- shoes
- boots
- gloves
- baseball glove
- equipment

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ make the shoes comfortable  
❌ use the shoes until they feel normal  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **break in the shoes**

---

### Main Sentence

**I need to break these shoes in before we run next week.**

---

### More Examples

👟 **Thể thao / chạy bộ**

- These shoes hurt — I need to **break them in**.  
- Run a few miles to **break your shoes in**.

---

🏈 **Sports**

- He spent weeks **breaking in his new gloves**.  

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- I’m still **breaking these in**.  
- These boots need to be **broken in**.

---

# 🎯 4. Break in (interrupt / broadcast interruption)

👉 **Break in = chen ngang chương trình / cắt ngang**

Hay dùng trong:

- TV news
- radio
- announcements

---

### Main Sentence

**The TV station broke in to report the president's death.**

---

### More Examples

📺 **Media**

- The news **broke in** with an emergency report.  
- The program was **interrupted** when the station **broke in**.

---

### Conversation Use (đời thường)

Trong đời sống thường nói:

- **Sorry to break in**, but I have something important.

Ví dụ:

- Sorry to **break in**, but the meeting starts in five minutes.

---

# 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **break in** → đột nhập / chen ngang  
- **break into** → phá cửa vào  
- **rescue** → giải cứu  
- **stole** → đã ăn trộm  
- **interrupt** → ngắt lời
