# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Bring up / Bring down

---

# 🎯 1. Bring somebody down (make someone feel sad)

👉 **Bring someone down = làm ai đó tụt mood / buồn / mất năng lượng**

Thường dùng khi:

- music
- news
- negative people
- depressing topics

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ make me unhappy  
❌ make me feel sad  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **bring me down**

---

### Main Sentence

**This sad music is bringing me down.**

---

### More Examples

🎵 **Mood**

- That movie really **brought me down**.  
- Negative people always **bring me down**.

---

💬 **Đời sống**

- Man, this weather is **bringing me down**.  
- Don't let that comment **bring you down**.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- It's kinda **bringing me down**.  
- Don't let it **bring you down**.  
- That news really **brought me down**.

---

# 🎯 2. Bring somebody up (raise a child)

👉 **Bring someone up = nuôi dạy / nuôi lớn**

Dùng khi nói về:

- parents
- grandparents
- guardians
- childhood

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ raise me  
❌ take care of me when I was young  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **bring someone up**

---

### Main Sentence

**My grandparents brought me up after my parents died.**

---

### More Examples

👨‍👩‍👦 **Gia đình**

- My parents **brought me up** to respect others.  
- She was **brought up** in a small town.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- I was **brought up** in Hanoi.  
- My mom **brought us up** by herself.

---

# 🎯 3. Bring something up (start a topic)

👉 **Bring something up = nhắc tới / mở chủ đề**

Rất phổ biến trong:

- conversation
- meetings
- arguments
- awkward topics

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ start talking about  
❌ mention the topic  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **bring it up**

---

### Main Sentence

**My mother walks out of the room when my father brings up sports.**

---

### More Examples

💬 **Conversation**

- Why did you **bring that up**?  
- He **brought up** an interesting idea.

---

💼 **Work / meeting**

- She **brought up** the bug during the meeting.  
- Someone **brought up** the performance issue.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- Don't **bring it up** again.  
- I didn't want to **bring this up**, but...

---

# 🎯 4. Bring something up (vomit)

👉 **Bring something up = nôn ra**

⚠️ Đây là **cách nói nhẹ nhàng / polite** cho **vomit**.

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ vomit  
❌ throw up food  

👉 Native đôi khi nói:

✅ **bring it up**

---

### Main Sentence

**He drank so much that he brought his dinner up.**

---

### More Examples

🤢 **Health**

- The baby **brought up** some milk.  
- He felt sick and **brought up** his lunch.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- I thought I was going to **bring up my lunch**.  
- The kid **brought it up** after eating too fast.

---

# 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **bring up** → nuôi dạy / nhắc tới / nôn  
- **bring down** → làm tụt mood  
- **respect** → tôn trọng  
- **topic** → chủ đề
