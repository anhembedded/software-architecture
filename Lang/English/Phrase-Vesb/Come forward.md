# 🎯 1. Come forward (volunteer information / evidence)

👉 **Come forward = tự nguyện đứng ra cung cấp thông tin**

Thường dùng trong:

- police investigations
- witnesses
- public announcements

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ volunteer information  
❌ provide evidence voluntarily  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **come forward**

---

### Main Sentence

**The woman came forward with her husband's fingerprints.**

---

### More Examples

🚓 **Crime / police**

- A witness **came forward** after the accident.  
- Police asked anyone with information to **come forward**.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- No one **came forward**.  
- A few witnesses finally **came forward**.

---
