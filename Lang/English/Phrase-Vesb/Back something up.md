# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Back up

## 🎯 1. Back something up (reverse / move backward)

👉 **Back up = lùi lại / đi lùi**

Thường dùng với:

- car
- truck
- step
- move

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ reverse the car  
❌ move the car backward  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **back up the car**

---

### Main Sentence

**You'll have to back up your car so I can get out.**

---

### More Examples

🚗 **Lái xe**

- Can you **back up a little**?  
- I need you to **back up** so I can park.  
- He **backed up** too fast and almost hit the wall.

---

🚶 **Di chuyển**

- **Back up a bit**, you're too close.  
- Everyone **back up**, give him space.

---

## 🎯 2. Back somebody up (support someone)

👉 **Back someone up = ủng hộ / đứng về phía ai**

Dùng khi:

- tranh luận
- họp
- quyết định quan trọng
- ai đó bị chất vấn

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ support me  
❌ agree with me  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **back me up**

---

### Main Sentence

**My wife backed me up on my decision to quit my job.**

⚠️ Native thường dùng **on** hoặc **over** decision.

---

### More Examples

👥 **Gia đình**

- My brother **backed me up** when we argued with our parents.  

---

💼 **Workplace**

- Thanks for **backing me up** in that meeting.  
- The team **backed him up** when the manager questioned his idea.

---

💻 **Dev / IT**

- The logs **back up** your theory.  
- The data **backs up** what you said.

(nghĩa này gần với **prove / support evidence**)

---

## 🎯 3. Native Conversation Lines

Mấy câu này cực phổ biến:

- **Can you back me up here?**  
- **Thanks for backing me up.**  
- **Just back up a little.**

---

## 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **back up** → lùi lại / hỗ trợ  
- **argument** → tranh luận  
- **decision** → quyết định  
- **support** → ủng hộ
