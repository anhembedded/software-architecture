# Am (A Minor)

> **A minor** is one of the most fundamental keys and chords on guitar.  
> It is the **relative minor of C major** (same notes, different tonic).

---

## 🎸 A Minor Chord (Am)

### Open Position (Standard)

```
e |--0--|  (E)
B |--1--|  (C)
G |--2--|  (A)
D |--2--|  (E)
A |--0--|  (A)
E |-----|  (muted or open E optional)
```

- Root note: **A**
- Notes: A – C – E (R – ♭3 – 5)
- A classic, moody open chord.

---

### Moveable Barre & CAGED Shapes

Learn Am in all 5 CAGED positions to own the fretboard.

- **E-shaped barre (5th fret)** – root on 6th string, 5th fret (A)
- **A-shaped barre (12th fret)** – root on 5th string, 12th fret (A)
- **C-shape** – rarely used as full barre, but essential for triads
- **G-shape** – root on 6th string, 5th fret (overlaps E shape)
- **D-shape** – root on 4th string, 7th fret

## 🎵 A Minor Scales

### A Natural Minor (Aeolian)

**Formula:** 2 1 2 2 1 2 2 (half steps)  
**Notes:** A – B – C – D – E – F – G – A

- Pattern 1 (E-shaped, 5th fret root):
  ```
  e |-----------------------------5-7-8--
  B |-----------------------5-6-8--------
  G |-----------------4-5-7--------------
  D |-----------5-7-9--------------------
  A |-----5-7-8--------------------------
  E |-5-7-8------------------------------
  ```

### A Minor Pentatonic

**Notes:** A – C – D – E – G  
**Position 1 (5th fret):**

```
e |--5---8--
B |--5---8--
G |--5---7--
D |--5---7--
A |--5---7--
E |--5---8--
```

This is the most used scale in rock and blues.

📁 _Related:_ [[Pentatonic Scales]]

---

### A Blues Scale

Adds the **♭5 (E♭)** to the minor pentatonic:  
A – C – D – **E♭** – E – G

```
e |--5---8--
B |--5---8--
G |--5-6-7--
D |--5---7--
A |--5-6-7--
E |--5---8--
```

The D#/Eb at fret 6 on the A string (or 8 on G string) is the “blue note”.

## 🎼 Key of A Minor – Diatonic Chords

Built from **A natural minor** (or harmonic/melodic minor for variety).

| Chord | Roman Numeral | Notes             |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| Am    | i             | A – C – E         |
| Bdim  | ii°           | B – D – F         |
| C     | III (♭III)    | C – E – G         |
| Dm    | iv            | D – F – A         |
| Em    | v             | E – G – B         |
| F     | VI (♭VI)      | F – A – C         |
| G     | VII (♭VII)    | G – B – D         |

*Using harmonic minor?* The V chord becomes **E major (E – G# – B)** for a strong dominant pull back to Am.

## 🔁 Common Progressions in Am

- **i – iv – v** (Am – Dm – Em) → classic minor
- **i – VI – III – VII** (Am – F – C – G) → “Andalusian” / pop minor
- **i – iv – VII – III** (Am – Dm – G – C) → uplifting yet sad
- **i – V – iv – VII** (Am – E – Dm – G) → with dominant E for tension
- **12-Bar Blues in Am:**  
  Am – Am – Am – Am – Dm – Dm – Am – Am – E – Dm – Am – E
