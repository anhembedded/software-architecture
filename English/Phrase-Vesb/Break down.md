# [[English-Daily]] - Phrasal Verb Upgrade: Break down

## 🎯 1. Break down (machine stops working)

👉 **Break down = hư / ngừng hoạt động (xe, máy móc)**

Thường dùng với:

- car
- truck
- engine
- machine
- system

---

### Thay vì nói (sách vở)

❌ the car stopped functioning  
❌ the machine stopped working  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **break down**

---

### Main Sentence

**Our car broke down on the highway during a snowstorm.**

---

### More Examples

🚗 **Xe cộ**

- My car **broke down** on the way to work.  
- His truck **broke down** in the middle of nowhere.  
- The engine **broke down** last night.

---

💻 **IT / Tech**

- The server **broke down** during peak traffic.  
- The system **broke down** after the update.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- My car **broke down** this morning.  
- The elevator **broke down** again.  
- Everything **broke down** at the worst time.

---

# 🎯 2. Break down (emotionally collapse)

👉 **Break down = suy sụp / bật khóc / mất kiểm soát cảm xúc**

Dùng khi ai đó:

- quá sốc
- quá buồn
- quá áp lực

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ she cried very hard  
❌ he became extremely upset  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **break down**

---

### Main Sentence

**The woman broke down when the police told her the news.**

---

### More Examples

💔 **Cảm xúc**

- He **broke down** after hearing the news.  
- She **broke down** in tears.  
- I almost **broke down** from the stress.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- He just **broke down**.  
- She **broke down crying**.  
- I thought he was going to **break down**.

---

# 🎯 3. Break something down (divide / simplify)

👉 **Break something down = chia nhỏ / giải thích dễ hiểu**

Dùng khi:

- giải thích vấn đề
- chia task
- phân tích dữ liệu

---

### Thay vì nói

❌ divide into parts  
❌ explain step by step  

👉 Native nói:

✅ **break it down**

---

### Main Sentence

**Our teacher broke the final project down into three parts.**

---

### More Examples

🎓 **Học tập**

- The teacher **broke the concept down** for us.  
- Let me **break this down** step by step.

---

💻 **IT / dev**

- Let's **break the problem down** into smaller tasks.  
- He **broke the algorithm down** so everyone could understand it.  
- We need to **break the system down** into modules.

---

### Native Conversation Lines

- Let me **break it down** for you.  
- Can you **break that down** again?  
- Let's **break this problem down**.

---

# 🛠 Key Vocabulary

- **break down** → hư / suy sụp  
- **in tears** → bật khóc  
- **step by step** → từng bước  
- **module** → thành phần hệ thống
