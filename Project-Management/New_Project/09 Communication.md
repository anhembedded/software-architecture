# 💬 Communicating with Your Manager Professionally

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> A professional engineer doesn't pretend to know everything. They clearly present **their level of understanding, their plan, and the risks**.

Prev: [[08 Pitfalls]] · Next: [[10 Uncertainty and Principles]]

---

## Situation A — You don't fully understand the requirement

Don't just say *"I don't understand the requirement."* Point to the decision that needs clarifying.

> [!quote] Suggested wording
> *"I understand the overall objective. I'd like to clarify a few details about the expected behavior and acceptance criteria before finalizing the implementation approach.*
>
> *I'll review the existing architecture in parallel and come back with the open questions and a proposed plan."*

You are not waiting idly. You handle what you can and identify what needs clarifying. → [[01 Clarify]]

## Situation B — You have never worked with the technology

Don't promise you can do it before you know the difficulty. Don't undersell yourself either.

> [!quote] Suggested wording
> *"I haven't worked with this framework before, so I'll first assess how it fits into our current system, verify the key technical dependencies, and identify any major risks.*
>
> *Once I have that initial assessment, I'll be able to propose a more reliable implementation plan and estimate."*

You commit to **a process that reduces uncertainty**, not to knowing everything in advance. → [[03 De-risk]]

## Situation C — You find a risk that may affect the deadline

Use six parts:

| # | Part | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 🔎 **Finding** | What did you discover? |
| 2 | 💥 **Impact** | How far does it reach? |
| 3 | 🧾 **Evidence** | What proof do you have now? |
| 4 | 🔀 **Options** | What are the ways to handle it? |
| 5 | 👍 **Recommendation** | Which one, and why? |
| 6 | 🗳️ **Decision needed** | What must the manager decide? |

> [!quote] Example
> *"The initial integration test revealed a compatibility issue with our current toolchain. This may affect the planned delivery date.*
>
> *I've confirmed the issue with a minimal build and identified two possible approaches: adapting the dependency or evaluating an alternative library. I'm assessing the impact of both options and will share a recommendation, including the trade-offs, before we commit to the implementation path."*

You bring **both the problem and the options**.

## 🧠 Remember

> **A:** *Clarify the decision point.* **B:** *Promise the process, not the answer.* **C:** *Finding → Impact → Evidence → Options → Recommendation → Decision.*
> Bring **options**, not only problems.
